# Shared Evaluation Datasets

All shared synthetic evaluation datasets are Apache-2.0 licensed and free of real personal data.

| Dataset | Purpose | Languages / Scope | Cases | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`multilingual_form_utterances.jsonl`](multilingual_form_utterances.jsonl) | Synthetic spoken utterances (romanized & native scripts) for form extraction | `en-IN`, `hi-IN`, `mr-IN`, `ta-IN`, `te-IN`, `bn-IN`, `gu-IN`, `kn-IN`, `ml-IN`, `pa-IN` | 12 | Apache-2.0 |
| [`indic_voice_queries.jsonl`](indic_voice_queries.jsonl) | Multilingual voice-first queries for agriculture, civic, banking & learning | 10 Indic + `en-IN` locales | 10 | Apache-2.0 |
| [`regulated_safety_prompts.jsonl`](regulated_safety_prompts.jsonl) | Adversarial prompt-injection and `S3`/`S4` human-approval gate checks | Safety & regulated compliance | 10 | Apache-2.0 |
